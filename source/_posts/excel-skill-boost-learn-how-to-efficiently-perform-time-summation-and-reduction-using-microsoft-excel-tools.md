##  Add Times in Microsoft Excel

 You might be keeping track of the time you spend working on a task or project. When that work is complete, you'll want the total time you spent. Whether for your own records or for billing a client, you can use a combination of formulas and formatting to get accurate time totals.

###  Format the Cells for Hours and Minutes

 Note that we'll be using the structure H:MM to stay consistent with the timekeeping of hours and minutes. While Excel should recognize this when you enter your data, you can make sure of it by formatting your cells.

[Select the cells](https://buynow-tips.techidaily.com/exploring-a-ravaged-world-on-motorcycle-in-days-gone-our-comprehhavis-review/) containing your entries and either right-click and pick "Format Cells" or go to the Home tab, click "Format" in the ribbon, and choose "Format Cells."

![On the Home tab, click Format, Format Cells](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/HomeFormatFormatCellsTimes-Excel.png) 

 In the Format Cells window, make sure that you're on the Number tab. Select "Custom" as the Category. Then, select "h:mm" from the list or enter the format into the Type box.

![Select the hour minute format](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/FormatNumberCustomHourMinute-Excel.png)

###  Add Times Using a Formula

 If you only have a couple of entries, you can enter a quick formula to add those hours and minutes.

 Select the cell where you want your time to go. Enter the following formula, replacing the cell references with your own.

=F2+F3

 Hit Enter to apply the formula and you should see your total hours and minutes for those entries.

![Add the formula to add the times](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/AddTimeHoursMinutes-Excel.png)

###  Add Times Using the Sum Function

 If your time-tracking sheet will contain many entries, using [the SUM function](https://instagram-videos.techidaily.com/updated-steps-to-instagram-verification-and-fan-growth-in-under-150-characters/) is the easiest way to go.

 Select the cell at the bottom of the entries. Go to the Home tab and click "Sum" (formerly labeled "AutoSum").

![On the Home tab, click Sum](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/AddTimeClickSum-Excel.png) 

 Excel should recognize the cells that you want to use. If not, simply drag through the cells containing your times to [populate the formula](https://win-solutions.techidaily.com/solving-the-d3d-initialization-error-fixing-errgfxd3dinit-on-gta-v/) and hit Enter.

![Select the cells for the formula](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/AddTimeSumCells-Excel.png) 

 You should see the total number of hours and minutes for your entries.

![Total hours and minutes](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/TotalTimeHoursMinutes-Excel.png) 

###  Format Times Using 24 Hours

 When you're adding entries that use 24-hour time, it's quite possible that you'll get an incorrect total. To avoid this, you can format the cell containing the result.

 Select the cell containing the formula. Either right-click and pick "Format Cells," or go to Home > Format > Format Cells.

 In the Format Cells window, make sure that the Number tab is selected. Pick "Custom" as the Category and select "\[h\]:mm;@" from the list, or enter the format into the Type box.

![Select the extended hour minute format](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/FormatNumberCustomExtendedHoursMinutes-Excel.png) 

 Click "OK" to apply the format to the cell. You should then see accurate results for entries using 24-hour times.

![Total hours and minutes using 24 hours](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/TotalTimeHoursMinutesUsing24-Excel.png)

###  Subtract Your Times

 Select the cell where you want your result and enter the following formula, replacing the cell references with your own.

=C2-B2

 Hit Enter to apply the formula.

 Next, you might need to format the cell for hours and minutes to show the total time spent. By default, your results might display as a time of day.

![Enter the formula to subtract the times](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/SubtractTimeStartEnd-Excel.png) 

 Select the cell containing the formula, right-click, and pick "Format Cells," or go to Home > Format > Format Cells.

 In the Format Cells window, on the Number tab, choose "Custom" as the category. Select "h:mm" or enter it into the Type box and click "OK."

![Select the hour minute format](https://static1.howtogeekimages.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/FormatNumberCustomHourMinute-Excel.png)
